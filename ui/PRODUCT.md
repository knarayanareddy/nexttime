# NextTime — Product Truth

## Audience

English-speaking adults learning Dutch who already know basic vocabulary but want to succeed in recurring real conversations. Users may feel anxious about mistakes and highly sensitive to surveillance, correction, or accent judgment.

## Core job

**Know the next useful thing to practice, complete a tiny rehearsal, and see whether the next authentic task went better.**

## Operating context

- iOS-first, one-handed use, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, local notifications, and push-to-talk.
- Private review of self excerpts and uncertain outcomes; no public performance metrics.
- Rehearsal immediately before a real event, with limited time and attention.

## Product personality

Quiet, respectful, private, and encouraging without cheerleading. A discreet pocket notebook and rehearsal companion—not a game, classroom gradebook, or always-listening coach.

## Voice

- `Next: confirm the repair appointment.`
- `Practice 90 seconds before Thursday's call.`
- `This outcome is unclear. Review it when you have a moment.`
- `You confirmed the time without switching languages this time.`

Avoid: `Fluency score`, `Native-like`, `Don't lose your streak`, `We heard everything`, or infantilizing praise.

## Durable constraints

- The next real moment dominates Today; lesson catalogs and streaks do not exist.
- Consent and source provenance are understandable in plain language.
- Review and correction are easy; uncertain ASR never looks like a learner failure.
- Real-world outcomes are visually distinct from rehearsal completion.
- Recording state is persistent and impossible to miss.

## Visual anti-references

- Comic typefaces, cartoon tutors, leaderboards, badges, flame streaks, or multicolor CEFR charts.
- Testimonial carousels and marketing-style social proof.
- Dense grammar correction markup and red error underlining everywhere.
- Hidden privacy controls or surveillance-like waveform backgrounds.

## Design success signals

- A learner can start the right rehearsal within two taps from Today.
- A learner can tell authentic outcomes from app practice and unknown from failure.
- Dynamic Type and VoiceOver preserve rehearsal, review, and privacy controls.
- The UI feels calming before a consequential conversation and does not reward oversharing.
